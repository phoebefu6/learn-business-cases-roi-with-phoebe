Learn Business Cases & ROI with Phoebe

A data or AI investment gets approved when a finance team believes the numbers, and most cases give them reasons not to: one option, one number, every benefit and no downside. These ten sessions build the other kind, a case a CFO will sign. Four Leader sessions are for the people who need the money: what a case is for, how a CFO reads NPV, IRR and payback, which assumption is doing the work, and what happens after the yes. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who build the model: only the cash that changes, time value and the hurdle rate, three verdicts side by side, swinging every assumption, the range and the option to wait, and a bench that computes all of it live in your browser. The running case, Keelson Mutual's claims fast-track triage, is constructed.
