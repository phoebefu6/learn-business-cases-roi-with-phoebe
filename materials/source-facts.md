## MUST FIX BEFORE PUBLISHING (CONTRADICTED / SUPERSEDED rows only)

1. **Item 1 - STPR 3.5% is SUPERSEDED.** The live Green Book (2026, last updated 28 Oct 2026, two days ago) sets 3.00% for years 1-30, 2.50% for 31-75, 2.25% for 76 onwards. 3.5/3.0/2.5 was the 2022 edition and the Feb 2026 edition. The reduced 1.5% health rate is also gone from the live text. Content must date-stamp the edition, or teach the formula and not the number.
2. **Item 3 - "must include do minimum" is CONTRADICTED.** Only business as usual MUST be shortlisted. A do minimum is one of the five options a shortlist "should typically include" (para 5.18). The options framework filter is "recommended" and "not mandatory".
3. **Item 8 - "place-based business cases" is not in the Green Book text.** The June 2025 review promised them; the 2026 Green Book and the 2026 Business Case Guidance contain no such phrase (grep, zero hits). Only "place-based analysis" (Green Book ch 7) exists. Do not teach place-based business cases as an implemented requirement.
4. **Item 14 - source tier caveat, not a numeric error.** All four numbers are right, but I read them in the authors' own manuscript of the article (arXiv), not the typeset HBR page (paywalled). Cite as HBR Sept 2011, vol 89 no 9, pp 23-25.
5. **Item 15 - attribution CORRECTED.** The "outside view" and planning-fallacy foundation is credited (by Flyvbjerg) to Kahneman and Tversky 1979, and Lovallo and Kahneman 2003. Kahneman and Lovallo 1993 is verified only for the "inside view" (abstract). Do not say 1993 introduced both views without a primary check.
6. **Item 17 - NANDA "95%" headline is wider than what the body measures; RAND "80%" was not measured by RAND (reported).** See rows.
